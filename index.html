The Essay So Far

You have the first sentence of your answer. It is not a fact about 1985. It is a claim, and the rest of the series will defend it.

Mikhail Gorbachev came to power in 1985 intending to save the Soviet system, not to end it. Perestroika and glasnost were meant to make communism work; instead they set loose forces he could not control, and the collapse of the Soviet system began with the attempt to save it.

Hold on to the shape of that sentence: an intention, then a result that ran the other way. Every strong answer to this question turns on that gap.
